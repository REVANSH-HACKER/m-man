The meaning

Why 73 miles?

Dr. Kalam spent his life proving that limits exist only until someone refuses to accept them. The 73-mile distance is our salute — a number that demands respect, split across the three disciplines that define endurance sport.

It is long enough to break you, and structured enough that — with the right training — you will finish. That contradiction is the whole point.

The course

Three legs to glory

A point-to-point coastal route designed to be tough, scenic and unforgettable.

01

Swim · 3 Miles

A single-loop open-water swim in calm, lifeguard-patrolled bay waters. Wetsuit-friendly, with kayaks every 200m and a generous cut-off.

02

Bike · 56 Miles

A fast, mostly-flat coastal highway loop with closed roads, three aid stations and ocean views the whole way.

03

Run · 14 Miles

A two-loop seafront run finishing on the red carpet beneath the Kalam memorial. Aid every mile and a roaring crowd to the line.
