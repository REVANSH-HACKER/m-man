Who we are

WE BUILD
DREAMERS INTO FINISHERS

The Missile Man is an endurance triathlon platform on a single mission: take everyday people who dare to dream, and carry them all the way to the finish line.

AK
Our inspiration

The man who taught a nation to dream

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — the Missile Man of India — rose from selling newspapers as a boy to becoming a leading aerospace scientist and the People's President. He believed greatness was not reserved for the gifted few, but earned by anyone willing to work, fail, and rise again.

We carry that same belief onto the swim line, the saddle and the road. Triathlon is hard. So is dreaming. We exist to help you do both — with structure, community and a relentless refusal to quit.

Our flagship 73 Mile Kalam Challenge is our tribute: a full endurance test that proves what an ordinary person can do when they decide not to stop.

What drives us

Our values

Four principles, borrowed from a scientist who never stopped reaching for the sky.

Dream Big

We start with an audacious goal and reverse-engineer the path. No dream is too far when the plan is right.

Show Up Daily

Discipline beats motivation. Small, consistent sessions compound into start-line confidence.

Rise Together

No one finishes alone. Coaches, volunteers and fellow athletes pull each other across the line.

Embrace Failure

F.A.I.L. = First Attempt In Learning. Every DNF is data. We turn setbacks into start lines.

Earn It

We don't sell shortcuts. The finish line is sweet precisely because the miles were hard.

Lead by Service

Greatness is measured by who you lift. Our athletes give back to the communities they race in.

The journey

From idea to ignition

2021

The spark

A handful of weekend triathletes asked a simple question: what if finishing was for everyone, not just the elite?

2023

The platform

We launched structured swim, bike and run programs — and the first 500 athletes crossed their first finish line.

2025

The challenge

The 73 Mile Kalam Challenge was born as our flagship tribute event, selling out its inaugural slots in days.

2026

The movement

9,000+ athletes, 18 race cities and a growing community proving that ordinary people do extraordinary things.
